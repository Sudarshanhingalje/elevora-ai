Privacy Policy for Elevora AI

Effective Date: June 2026

1. Overview

Elevora AI is a SaaS platform that enables users to generate, schedule, and publish content to social media platforms including Facebook and Instagram using official Meta APIs.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect the following data:

  • Facebook Page ID
  • Instagram Business Account ID
  • Access Tokens (securely stored)
  • Post content (text, captions, media prompts)
  • Scheduling information

3. How We Use Data

We use this data only for:

  • Publishing posts to social media platforms
  • Scheduling automated campaigns
  • Managing user-requested workflows

4. Data Storage & Security

All sensitive data such as access tokens are stored securely in backend systems and are never shared, sold, or exposed publicly.

5. Third-Party Services

We integrate with:

  • Meta (Facebook & Instagram APIs)
  • Cloud hosting providers (Vercel, Docker infrastructure)
  • Database services (MySQL, Redis)

6. User Responsibility

Users are responsible for ensuring they have permission to connect their Facebook/Instagram accounts.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their data by contacting support.
